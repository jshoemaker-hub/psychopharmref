Psychiatric Medication Price Comparison

Open the Price Comparison

Runs free in your browser. Prices load from a data file on the site; your searches are not sent to a server.

What it shows

The table lists cash (no insurance) prices for the psychiatric medications in the PsychoPharmRef Drug Database, grouped by category: antidepressants, antipsychotics, anxiolytics, mood stabilizers, stimulants, non-stimulant ADHD drugs, sleep medications, VMAT2 inhibitors, substance use treatments and others. Each row names the drug, its class, and the strength and quantity priced, based on usual dosing and roughly a 30-day supply. A search box filters by drug name.

Prices link to the seller's product page. A cell more than 30 days old shows its "as of" date, and the header shows when each source was last updated.

Reading the prices

NADAC is not a price a patient can buy at, so it is excluded from the Best column. Because it is an average of what many pharmacies pay, a low-markup seller like Cost Plus can come in below it for some generics. Some rows carry a note, for example for clinic- or REMS-only products, or for drugs with no NADAC listing. For brand-name drugs, check the manufacturer's savings program; retail cash prices appear only when a pharmacy stocks the product.

Limitations

Cash prices change often and may vary by pharmacy and zip code. Insurance copays or discount coupons may be lower. Verify current pricing with the pharmacy before counseling patients. Inclusion of a pharmacy does not imply endorsement, and the table is not medical or financial advice.
