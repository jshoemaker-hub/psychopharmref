Educational reference only. Molecular weight, formula, cLogP, polar surface area, and aqueous solubility (ESOL model) are computed with RDKit from each drug’s SMILES; structural similarity is a Morgan/ECFP4 Tanimoto score; the 3D view is a single computed conformer. Aqueous solubility is the estimated intrinsic (neutral-form) value — marketed salt forms are far more soluble. pKa values are approximate literature figures used to estimate ionization at pH 7.4. Region annotations and “generation evolution” notes are curated teaching summaries, not a substitute for primary medicinal-chemistry sources.
