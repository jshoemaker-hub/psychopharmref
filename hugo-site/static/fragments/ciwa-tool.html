Clinical Use: CIWA-Ar is validated for adults with known alcohol withdrawal; it assumes the patient can communicate and is not heavily sedated. Patients with impaired consciousness, acute medical illness, or severe comorbidity (e.g., hepatic encephalopathy, head trauma) may require fixed-dose regimens instead of symptom-triggered protocols. Scores >15 indicate severe withdrawal with increased risk of seizures and delirium tremens; treat promptly and monitor closely.
