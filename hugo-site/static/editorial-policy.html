Editorial policy

How this site is written, dated, and corrected.

PsychoPharmRef is a free educational reference run by Jerad Shoemaker, MD, a psychiatrist. Chapters and tools are for clinicians and trainees. This page describes the practice already visible on the site. It does not add a peer-review board, a hospital affiliation, or credentials beyond that.

Chapters

Clinical chapters name Jerad Shoemaker, MD in the byline. The published month on a chapter is the publication date stored with that chapter. When a chapter also shows an updated date, that date is the last time the chapter text changed. The site does not keep a separate “last medically reviewed” date.

Sources are cited in the chapter and, where the chapter has one, in a reference list on that page. There is no single bibliography for the whole site. How the drug table’s receptor and safety fields are sourced is described in References & Methods.

Newsroom

Newsroom pieces use house pen names: Marion Hale, Theo Whitcombe, Iris Calder, Edith Marrow, Julian Ashby, and Nora Quill. Those are not real people. Each news page says that pieces are drafted with AI assistance from the cited sources and edited by Jerad Shoemaker, MD. The publication date and the source list are on the page.

Tools and handouts

Calculators and rating scales run in the browser. Patient handouts are written for patients and caregivers and are separate from the clinician chapters. Limits of the reference are in the legal disclaimer.

Advertising and the newsletter

The site does not run advertising. The newsletter is hosted on beehiiv. Issues are not copied onto the site as a second version of a chapter. Past issues stay on beehiiv.

Corrections

If a page is wrong, use the contact form. Personal-information requests are described in the privacy policy.
