Managing Stress & Preventing Burnout

Stress is normal and even useful in short bursts. Burnout is what happens when the stress never switches off. Here's how to spot it early and recover.

🔥

Stress vs. Burnout

Stress is too much: over-engaged, urgent, "I can't keep up." Burnout is empty: disengaged, numb, "what's the point."

Burnout builds slowly from chronic demands without enough recovery. Catching it early makes it far easier to reverse.

⚠

Warning Signs

  • Exhaustion that sleep doesn't fix
  • Cynicism, irritability, or dread
  • Trouble concentrating; work feels pointless
  • Headaches, gut trouble, getting sick often
  • Pulling away from people and things you enjoy
⚖

The Recovery Equation

Burnout isn't just about the load — it's the balance of demands vs. recovery. You lower it two ways:

  • Reduce demands: say no, delegate, simplify, drop what isn't essential.
  • Increase recovery: real rest, sleep, connection, movement, joy.
🔄

Recharge Daily

  • Take true breaks — away from screens, not just switching tasks.
  • Protect sleep; it's the master recovery tool.
  • Move your body to discharge stress chemicals.
  • Do one thing purely because you enjoy it.
🚫

Protect Your Limits

  • Decide when the workday ends — and honor it.
  • Practice saying "I can't take that on right now."
  • Batch worries into a set "worry time" instead of all day.
  • Ask for help before you're at the end of your rope.
