MAO Inhibitors (MAOIs)

Includes phenelzine, tranylcypromine, isocarboxazid, and selegiline. Powerful antidepressants that require a special diet and careful drug avoidance.

💊

What This Group Is

MAOIs are strong antidepressants, often used when other medicines haven’t worked.

They require a special diet and strict attention to other medications, so they are reserved for people who can follow these rules carefully.

🍽

The Tyramine Diet (Very Important)

MAOIs react with tyramine, a substance in aged and fermented foods, which can cause a dangerous blood-pressure spike.

Avoid: aged cheeses, cured/smoked meats, tap or unpasteurized beer, soy sauce, sauerkraut, and other fermented foods. Your prescriber will give you a full list.

🛑

Black Box Warning

Like all antidepressants, MAOIs carry a small risk of increased suicidal thoughts in people under 25. Watch closely early in treatment.

🩹

Common (Usually Mild) Side Effects

  • Dizziness on standing (low blood pressure).
  • Trouble sleeping.
  • Weight gain.
  • Sexual side effects.
⚠

Serious — Seek Care Now

  • Hypertensive crisis: sudden pounding headache, stiff neck, sweating, nausea — a medical emergency.
  • Serotonin syndrome: from combining with SSRIs, SNRIs, tramadol, triptans, and many others.
  • Fainting from low blood pressure.
🚫

Alcohol & Drug Interactions

Avoid alcohol, especially tap beer and red wine (tyramine).

Never combine with other antidepressants, decongestants (pseudoephedrine), stimulants, meperidine, or tramadol. A washout period (up to 2 weeks, or 5 weeks after fluoxetine) is required before switching.

🧪

Monitoring

We check your blood pressure and review your diet and medications at each visit.

Keep an up-to-date list and tell every provider (including dentists and ER staff) that you take an MAOI.

This is general information, not a substitute for your prescriber's advice. Never start, stop, or change a dose on your own — some of these medicines need a slow, supervised taper. Bring this sheet to your appointments and ask questions.
