Diet & Exercise: The Basic Science

Your brain runs on your body. How you move and eat shapes your mood, energy, and stress — not as a cure, but as a foundation everything else builds on.

🏃

Why Exercise Helps Mood

Movement releases endorphins and BDNF, a protein that helps brain cells grow and connect. It also burns off stress hormones and improves sleep.

Studies show regular activity can ease mild-to-moderate depression and anxiety — sometimes comparably to other treatments.

💪

How Much Is Enough

  • Aim for about 150 minutes/week of moderate activity — roughly 30 min, 5 days.
  • "Moderate" = you can talk but not sing (brisk walk counts).
  • Something beats nothing. Even 10 minutes lifts mood.
  • Add light strength work twice a week if you can.
🧠

Food & the Brain

  • The brain uses ~20% of your energy — it needs steady fuel.
  • Blood-sugar spikes and crashes swing mood and energy.
  • Most of your serotonin is made in the gut, so gut health matters.
🥐

Simple Nutrition Wins

  • Eat regularly — skipping meals worsens mood and focus.
  • Build plates around protein, fiber, vegetables, and whole grains.
  • Include omega-3s (fish, walnuts, flax).
  • Stay hydrated; even mild dehydration dents concentration.
☕

Watch These

  • Caffeine late in the day fuels anxiety and wrecks sleep.
  • Alcohol is a depressant and fragments sleep.
  • Lots of ultra-processed food and added sugar is linked to lower mood.
