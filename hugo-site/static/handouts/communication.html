Assertive Communication

Assertive means honest and respectful — standing up for yourself while still respecting the other person. It's the middle path between bottling things up and blowing up.

⚖

Three Styles

  • Passive: your needs lose. You go along, then resent it.
  • Aggressive: their needs lose. You win the moment but damage trust.
  • Assertive: both count. Clear, calm, respectful — the goal.
👤

Use "I" Statements

"You" statements feel like blame and invite defensiveness. "I" statements share your experience:

"I feel ___ when ___ because ___. I'd like ___."

"I feel overwhelmed when the dishes pile up because I'm already stretched. I'd like us to split them."

👂

Listen, Too

  • Let them finish before you respond.
  • Reflect back: "So you're saying…"
  • You can understand someone without agreeing.
  • Aim to solve the problem, not win the argument.
🧠

Stay Steady

  • Calm voice, steady eye contact, relaxed posture.
  • Slow down — a breath before you answer.
  • Stick to one issue; don't pile on old ones.
  • If it's heating up, pause: "Let's take a break and come back."
💬

Handy Phrases

  • "I see it differently. Here's how…"
  • "I need some time to think about that."
  • "That doesn't work for me."
  • "I hear you, and I still feel…"
