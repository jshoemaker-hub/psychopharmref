Bupropion (Wellbutrin)

An antidepressant that also helps with energy, focus, and quitting smoking. It does not usually cause weight gain or sexual side effects.

💊

What It Is

Bupropion works on dopamine and norepinephrine rather than serotonin. That’s why it tends to be activating — it can boost energy and focus.

It treats depression and helps people stop smoking (sold as Zyban for that use).

🍽

How to Take It

Take in the morning (and early afternoon for twice-daily forms) — late doses can cause insomnia.

Take with or without food, but be consistent. Swallow XL/SR tablets whole; do not crush or chew.

🛑

Black Box Warning

As with all antidepressants, there is a small risk of increased suicidal thoughts in people under 25 early in treatment. Watch closely and call if mood worsens.

🩹

Common (Usually Mild) Side Effects

  • Dry mouth, headache.
  • Trouble sleeping — take earlier in the day.
  • Feeling jittery, restless, or anxious.
  • Nausea, mild tremor.
⚠

Serious — Call Us or Seek Care

  • Seizures — rare but dose-related. Risk goes up with high doses, alcohol, or an eating disorder.
  • Raised blood pressure.
  • Severe agitation, confusion, or a “high”/racing mood.
  • Allergic reaction: rash, hives, swelling.
🚫

Who Should Avoid It & Alcohol

Do not take if you have a seizure disorder or active/past bulimia or anorexia — these raise seizure risk.

Avoid alcohol, and never stop heavy drinking or benzodiazepines abruptly while on it — both can trigger seizures.

🧪

Monitoring

We may check your blood pressure during treatment.

No routine blood tests are needed.
