Healthy Habits & Gratitude

Wellbeing is built from small routines repeated daily. A steady rhythm and a habit of noticing the good gently tilt your baseline toward better.

🔄

Anchor Your Day

A few fixed points give the day structure and your brain a sense of safety. You don't need a rigid schedule — just reliable anchors:

  • A consistent wake and sleep time
  • Regular meals
  • Some daily movement and daylight
🧩

Habit Stacking

The easiest new habit attaches to one you already have:

"After I ___, I will ___."

"After I pour my coffee, I'll write one thing I'm grateful for." The old habit becomes the reminder.

🌱

Make Habits Stick

  • Start tiny — two minutes counts.
  • Same time, same trigger, every day.
  • Make it obvious: lay out the shoes, leave the journal open.
  • Missed a day? Never miss twice. Just restart.
🧠

Why Gratitude Works

The brain has a built-in negativity bias — it scans for threats and skips the good. Gratitude deliberately trains attention toward what's going right, which research links to better mood and sleep.

It's not toxic positivity — hard things stay hard. It just widens the lens.

🙏

A Simple Practice

  • Each night, write 3 good things from the day.
  • Be specific: "the sun on my walk," not "nature."
  • Add why it mattered or your part in it.
  • Small counts — a good coffee, a kind text.
