Managing Challenging Behaviors

Difficult behaviors are usually the person's way of saying something is wrong. Look for the unmet need behind the behavior, and it becomes far easier to ease.

🔑

The ABC Approach

  • A — Antecedent: what happened just before? (noise, hunger, a question)
  • B — Behavior: what exactly did they do?
  • C — Consequence: what happened after, and did it help?

Spotting the pattern points you to the trigger to change.

🔍

Check Unmet Needs First

Before anything else, rule out the basics:

  • Pain, hunger, thirst, or needing the bathroom
  • Too hot, cold, tired, or overstimulated
  • Boredom or loneliness
  • A new medication or infection
🔥

Agitation & Aggression

  • Stay calm; keep your voice low and body relaxed.
  • Don't argue or restrain — give space and safety.
  • Redirect to a soothing activity or another room.
  • Reduce triggers: noise, crowds, rushing, being quizzed.
🌅

Sundowning

Late-day confusion and restlessness are common. To ease it:

  • Keep afternoons calm; limit naps and caffeine.
  • Turn on lights before dusk to cut shadows.
  • Hold a soothing evening routine.
  • Plan demanding tasks for the morning.
🚶

Wandering & Repetition

  • Wandering often means restlessness or looking for something — offer a walk or a task.
  • Ensure doors are secured and they carry ID.
  • For repeated questions, answer calmly and briefly, then redirect.
  • Try not to show frustration — each time feels new to them.
