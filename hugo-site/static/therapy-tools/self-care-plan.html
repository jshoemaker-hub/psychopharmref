My Self-Care Assessment & Plan

Self-care isn't a luxury — it's the maintenance that keeps you well. Rate how you're doing in each area, then pick one small change per area to start.

🧠

How Self-Care Works

Think of yourself like a phone battery. Daily demands drain you; self-care recharges you. When you run at 0% for too long, everything — mood, patience, health — suffers.

Good self-care is small, regular, and realistic, not occasional and grand. Consistency beats intensity.

Make It Stick

  • Attach it to something you already do (e.g., stretch after brushing teeth).
  • Start absurdly small — a 5-minute walk counts.
  • Track it where you'll see it.
  • Missed a day? Just begin again. No streak required.
Your lowest-scoring area is your best starting point — not because you've failed there, but because a small change will help the most. Pick just one. You can always add more later.
