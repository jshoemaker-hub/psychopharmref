BHI Medication Reference — Prototype

This is exactly how the new BHI Ref column will behave in your Drug Database: click View / Copy on any row and the reference card pops out, with a one-click Copy for EMR button that puts a clean plain-text version on your clipboard. Each card combines FDA-package-insert dosing prose (starting / stopping / missed doses / converting) with FDA black box warnings, mechanism, indications, dose adjustments, pregnancy & lactation, and common side effects pulled live from your existing database. All 91 medications in the database now have an entry (the 4 originals link to direct FDA PDFs; the rest link to the official DailyMed label). Click any row to review.
